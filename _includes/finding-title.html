{%- comment -%} Advisory titles read "CVE-x: Product - Description". The CVE and vendor are displayed separately everywhere we use this, so strip both prefixes and output just the description. Research titles have neither, so this is a no-op there. Usage: {% capture label %}{% include finding-title.html post=post %}{% endcapture %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign p = include.post -%} {%- assign disp = p.title -%} {%- if p.cve and disp contains p.cve -%} {%- assign disp = disp | remove_first: p.cve | remove_first: ":" | strip -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign parts = disp | split: " - " -%} {%- if parts.size > 1 -%} {%- assign lead = parts[0] -%} {%- assign lead_len = lead | size -%} {%- if lead_len < 60 -%} {%- assign disp = disp | remove_first: lead | remove_first: " - " -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endif -%} {{- disp -}}
